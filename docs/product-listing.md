## Summary

Diony AI is a desktop film studio for Mac and Windows that shoots on cloud video models. Type one line; the Director writes the script, prices every shot before anything rolls, films the clips on Grok Imagine or Google Flow, reviews each print, cuts and narrates the film, and hands you the master with subtitles, an edit list, a QC packet, and the receipt. No GPU. Your own provider keys, never uploaded anywhere but the provider you chose. Every clip and film lands in a vault on your machine the moment it exists.

## Additional details

**One line in, a priced film out**
Describe the film in a sentence, or paste your own script. The Director drafts the screenplay, derives the exact prompt every clip inherits, and shows the itemised price before a single frame renders.

**Two cameras, one look per film**
Grok Imagine (xAI, metered per second, 15 s takes, native audio) or Google Flow (Veo 3.1 and Omni Flash via useapi.net, priced in Google's own credits, free 1080p finish). One camera shoots the whole film so every shot carries one look.

**Nothing rolls before you see the price**
Clips, plates, character sheets, and a retake allowance are itemised at the lock. The header meter reads in the camera's own currency, dollars or credits. A failed clip that cost money stays on the receipt.

**Faces and continuity that hold**
Cast Lock keeps an approved identity on every prompt. A film-level look bible, style frames, and chained clips that open on the previous last frame keep the world consistent across the cut.

**A finished master, not a folder of clips**
Reviewed, assembled with fades and a room-tone floor, loudness-locked, and narrated in post by one cast voice. Delivered as film.mp4 with SRT and VTT subtitles, an edit list, an assembly map, and a QC packet.

**Your keys, your vault, your machine**
Bring an xAI key, or a useapi.net token with a Gemini key. Keys stay in one file on your desk. Media is downloaded and content-hashed the moment it completes; provider links are never stored.

**Ready on Mac and Windows**
ffmpeg and the runtime come inside the app. Open it, put your keys in one file, choose a camera, and press Continue. No Python, no terminal, no package manager.
